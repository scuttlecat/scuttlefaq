---
title: "How do I get Mythic Essence?"
summary: "25 in the paid pass. Another 25 if you pay another 2000 RP for the second pass bundle. 125 in the HoL pass once a year. Otherwise, gamble."
category: "Shops & Currency"
order: 3
---
There is always a pass going on. The paid version has 25 ME in it. There are two additional bundles that both also contain the pass, and the more expensive one (2000 RP on top of the pass price) has another 25 ME when purchased. The last pass of the year has 50 ME instead of 25. The Hall of Legends pass, which is once a year, has 125 ME and is 1950 RP. Most Sanctum rolls will give you some ME, but the amounts are usually small. Orbs can rarely drop 10 ME.
