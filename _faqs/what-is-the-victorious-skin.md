End a season with honor 3 and 15 ranked wins to get the Victorious skin. The skin for season 3 2026 is Victorious Riven and will be distributed during the first patch of 2027. Victorious Skins are never available after the season they are released. Timeless versions of Victorious skins will be available in 2027's Ranked Rush. At this time from available information you will be limited to acquiring 3 Timeless Victorious skins. Timeless versions are different skins and are not chromas.
